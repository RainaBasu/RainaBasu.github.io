Who I Am

A little bit
about me

I’m a Computer Science graduate, currently pursuing my MS in Intelligent Systems at the University of Pittsburgh. Most of my projects seem to begin with me wondering, “Wait, but why does it do that?” and then spending far too long trying to find out. That curiosity eventually led me to machine learning. I like taking a problem apart, experimenting with different approaches, and understanding what makes a model succeed, fail, or behave unexpectedly. Along the way, I’ve worked on everything from deepfake detection and AI interview systems to fashion analytics. I especially enjoy studying the data and figuring out what approach makes the most sense for it. There’s something satisfying about trying different models, seeing how they behave, and slowly putting the pieces together. Almost like solving a puzzle!

MIA — Mock Interview Agent
Python, React, LangGraph, Azure OpenAI, OpenSearch Jan 2025 – Apr 2025
  • Built an agentic AI mock interview system with a LangGraph adaptive pipeline — nodes for scoring, retrieval, difficulty adaptation, question generation, and validation.
  • Implemented RAG using OpenSearch kNN vector search over a curated SE interview Q&A corpus to ground GPT-generated questions and reduce hallucinations.
  • Integrated Azure Speech TTS/STT for fully spoken interaction and a React + Vite frontend with rubric-based post-session feedback reports via FastAPI.
Deepfake Detection System
Python, TensorFlow, OpenCV · EfficientNet-LSTM Jul 2024 – Jun 2025
  • Designed a hybrid EfficientNetB0 + LSTM architecture for spatial-temporal deepfake detection on Celeb-DF V1 & V2 datasets.
  • Achieved 95.8% validation accuracy, 95.81% precision, and 95.86% recall on Celeb-DF V2, demonstrating strong generalization on high-quality deepfakes.
  • Built an end-to-end video pipeline with a Flask inference API.
Published — “Deepfake Detection using Hybrid Model for Trust of Citizens,” ICDLAIR 2024, Atlantis Press. Read the paper →
Fashion Trend Analysis & Outfit Classification
Python, OpenCV, ML Models Jan 2025 – Apr 2025
  • Built a computer vision and ML pipeline on 10,000+ fashion images to classify regional and seasonal attire trends across 4 major metropolitan markets.
  • Implemented ROI bounding-box image processing to extract dominant RGB color spaces, garment textures, and pattern features.
  • Benchmarked Random Forest, LSTM, and SVM models; peak performance of 70.23% accuracy and 65.78% precision with Random Forest.
Published — “Analysis and Prediction of Woman’s Dress Categories from Fashion Trends,” ICCUBEA 2025, IEEE. Read the paper →
LLM-Based Movie Summarization
Python, Llama 3.1, DeepSeek-R1, NLP 2025
  • Developed an LLM-based abstractive summarization pipeline generating movie plot summaries directly from full subtitle transcripts using Llama 3.1 and DeepSeek-R1.
  • Evaluated generated summaries against reference plots using ROUGE-1, ROUGE-2, ROUGE-L, and SummaC for quantitative comparison of semantic consistency and quality across models.
